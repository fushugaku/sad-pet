Original procedural expansion (20 sounds, version 0.17)
------------------------------------------------------
Lo-fi beat, Breakbeat, Four on the floor, Electro percussion, Dub chords,
Acid line, FM marimba, Kalimba garden, Nylon harmonics, Music box, Tape piano,
String orbit, Shimmer pad, Dark drone, Singing bowl, Resonant bells,
Circuit glitches, Space transmission, Ocean swell, Vinyl dust.

Generated entirely by project-authored synthesis and note/event patterns in
rust/src/samples/bank.rs. No third-party sound recordings or melodies are used
for these additions. Noise is deterministic; instrument strikes, resonances,
drum synthesis, textures and stereo echoes are calculated locally in Rust.
Rhythmic entries last exactly 16 beats (four bars in 4/4) before frame rounding.
Events and their release/echo tails wrap around the loop instead of truncating
the end or shortening the musical duration. Each WAV is stereo 48 kHz float,
DC-corrected and peak-normalized to 0.78, saved in the user's IndexedDB library
and available for download through the same controls as the existing sources.

Original categorized expansion (128 sounds)
-------------------------------------------
Sixteen sources in each of Drums, Percussion, Vocals, Bass, Keys,
Plucks & mallets, Pads & strings, and Textures & FX. Names, descriptions,
tempos and the complete original arrangements are in:
  rust/src/samples/bank/expansion.rs

All audio is synthesized locally by project-authored Rust code. Vocals are
wordless formant synthesis (including humming, chants, breathy phrases and
layered harmonies), not human recordings or voicebank excerpts. Named acoustic
instruments and environmental sounds describe synthesized approximations.
No third-party recordings, soundfonts or existing compositions are used.
The sources vary in rhythm, melody, register, synthesis and articulation.
Every addition is a four-bar stereo loop, rendered at 48 kHz on demand,
with circular tails, DC removal and a peak ceiling of 0.78. The preceding
30 entries retain their indices, factory IDs and audio generators.

Recorded musical loops (256 sources, CC0)
----------------------------------------
Source: Freesound Loop Dataset, Ramires et al., ISMIR 2020.
Dataset DOI / download: https://zenodo.org/records/3967852
Archive MD5: c260a1203a82c1dda80ec1de75e5e23f (FSL10K.zip).

These are multi-beat musical loops, 4–24 seconds each, not individual note or
drum-hit samples. Each selected sound has a CC0 dedication in the dataset's
per-sound metadata and a well-cut / defined-tempo annotation. The bank covers
drum grooves, percussion patterns, bass lines, keys, guitar/plucked phrases,
synth lines, vocal/beatbox loops, pads, textures and mixed musical tracks.
Selected WAV bytes are preserved. Display titles and filenames are cleaned up;
full original names, authors, source links, annotations, SHA-256 fingerprints
and technical measurements are retained in loops-256/manifest.json.

Per-source credits: loops-256/CREDITS.txt
License text: loops-256/LICENSE-CC0.txt
Verification: python3 rust/scripts/check_loop_bank.py
No external account or runtime request to Freesound/Zenodo is required.
Browsers fetch the selected WAV from the app's own static assets. Native
builds embed the recordings; the WAV bank is excluded from the WASM binary.

Sampler starter packs (8 kits, CC0)
-----------------------------------
Folder: rust/assets/sampler-packs (index packs.json; per pack pack.json, samples/,
CREDITS.txt with the original file of every sample).
Sources, pinned to a commit:
  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), Versilian Studios LLC, CC0 1.0:
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL (c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e)
  Roland TR-808 Sound Sample Set 1.0.0, Michael Fischer (Technopolis, 1994),
  released free of charge by its author and dedicated CC0 1.0 by TidalCycles:
  https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer (85fbecf1bec32553395625ea659e2a56dfd7c0e1)
Files are trimmed of silence, faded, capped in length and converted to 16-bit
44.1 kHz WAV with one gain per pad. Rebuild: python3 rust/scripts/build_sampler_packs.py
--cache <folder>; verify: node rust/scripts/check_sampler_packs.mjs (index versions,
files, and every keygroup root against the engine's pitch detector).
The web build serves the folder as static files; the editor copies a pack into
IndexedDB the first time it loads.
