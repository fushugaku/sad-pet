TR-808 — a SAD PET Sampler pack
========================================

Source: Roland TR-808 Sound Sample Set 1.0.0, Michael Fischer (Technopolis, 1994), published by TidalCycles
  https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (repository dedication; the author released the set free of charge)
Pinned at: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer/85fbecf1bec32553395625ea659e2a56dfd7c0e1/
