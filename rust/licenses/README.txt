MP3 export includes LAME 3.100 and the Rust wrappers mp3lame-encoder 0.2.5 and mp3lame-sys 0.1.11.
Copyrights and license terms are preserved in the accompanying files.
Sources: https://github.com/DoumanAsh/mp3lame-encoder and https://github.com/DoumanAsh/mp3lame-sys
LAME: https://lame.sourceforge.io/
The wrappers and bundled LAME build are fetched as Cargo sources; Cargo.lock pins their exact versions.
